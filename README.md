# OnsAms (beta)

*For Amsterdam, by everyone.* A concept prototype of a social currency for Amsterdam. Visitors earn points by doing things that give back and spend them with local places, makers and neighbours; residents host experiences and vote on what their neighbourhood fund pays for.

**Try it:** https://aohi-consulting.github.io/onsams/ (best on a phone)

Built by Team 5 (Residents) for the Regenerative Design course at Hotelschool The Hague, on Lin's original OnsAms design. This is a student prototype: places and organisations are real Amsterdam examples, not partners; points, prices and people are illustrative; nothing you enter leaves your device.
